Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a love of homemade bread and a wish to bring neighbors together. We wanted to create a welcoming place where people could stop for breakfast or find something to share.

Today, we bring that same care to our breads, pastries, cookies, and celebration cakes. We are happy to be part of our customers' everyday routines and special occasions.

Our Ingredients

We choose ingredients for their quality and flavor. When available, we buy seasonal ingredients from local suppliers.

We prepare our baked goods in small batches. If you have a food allergy, please ask about ingredients and shared equipment before ordering.

Meet Our Team

Our Bread Baker

Our bread baker prepares the dough and watches each batch as it bakes, giving every loaf care from mixing to cooling.

Our Pastry and Cake Baker

Our pastry and cake baker prepares sweet treats and helps customers plan cakes for their celebrations.

Our Front Counter Team

Our counter team welcomes visitors, answers questions, and helps arrange preorder pickups.
